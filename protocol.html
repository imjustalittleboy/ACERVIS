The ACERVIS Protocol

1. Introduction

The ACERVIS protocol is a decentralized infrastructure designed to anchor academic credentials to the Polygon blockchain. It solves the "Double-Spend of Trust" in Nigerian education by ensuring that once a degree is issued, its cryptographic footprint is immutable and globally verifiable.

2. Cryptographic Synthesis

Every credential is reduced to a deterministic SHA-256 hash using the formula:

D = H( P ‖ σ ‖ ρ )

Where P is the student metadata, σ is a row-level salt, and ρ is a global pepper. This 3-tier entropy ensures that even if the database is breached, the blockchain anchors cannot be spoofed.

3. Institutional Governance

The protocol distinguishes between Super Admins (Governance) and Institutions (Issuers). Institutions are granted issuance quotas by the Super Admin, preventing a single entity from flooding the network.

4. Blockchain Layer

We utilize Polygon L2 for its high throughput and low gas fees. The AcervisRegistry.sol contract maintains the state of all anchored credentials, supporting both permanent revocation and temporary suspension.
